Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up a Hover Box
Add a hover box to your site to create a cool, interactive effect for your site visitors.Hover boxes change when visitors hover over them to reveal different elements and content. You can also add animation to the elements to further wow visitors, and encourage them to continue exploring your site.Step 1 | Add a hover box to your siteAdd a hover box to begin building eye-catching pages. There are lots of fun, colorful templates available, so you can choose one to match your site's look and theme.To add a hover box:
Go to your editor. Click Add Elements  on the left side of the editor. Click Interactive. Click Hover Boxes. Click or drag your chosen box to add it to the page. Step 2 | Add elements to your hover boxAdd elements to your box to display relevant information and content for your visitors. You can add different elements for regular and hover mode, or both.To add an element to both views, simply add it on Regular mode. To only display an element on Regular mode, add it, then hide it on Hover mode. To display an element only in Hover, just add it on Hover mode.To add elements to your hover box:
Click the hover box. Click either the Regular, or Hover tab, depending on which mode you'd like to add the element to:
    
Regular: 

Click Add on the left side of the editor. Click or drag your chosen element to add it to your box.   Hover:

Click Add to Hover on the left side of the editor. Click or drag your chosen element to add it to the box.    Note:If you add a button in regular view, you can't edit the design in hover mode. As a workaround, you can hide the button in hover view, then re-add it and design it to your needs.Step 3 | Customize the box designYou can customize the hover box to look exactly the way you want. Depending on the box design you select, you can adjust settings such as the fill, border, and corners to suit your site's needs. To customize the box design:
Click the Design  icon. Click Customize Design. (Optional) Select a different preset design at the top. Choose from the following tabs to adjust each setting:
    
Fill Color and Opacity: Choose a background color and  drag the slider to adjust its opacity. Border: Adjust the border's color, opacity, and width. Corners: Use the boxes to adjust the pixel size of the box's corners. Shadow: Enable or disable a shadow, and adjust the angle, distance, size blur, color, and opacity.  Step 4 | Add an effect to your hover boxEffects bring your Hover Boxes to life. With a range of choices available, you can create an effect that is both eye-catching and fun.You can also customize the effect to perfectly suit your site and hover box.To add and customize an effect:
Click the hover box. Click the Hover tab. Click Choose Effect. Click Customize the adjust the effect's settings.Click an option below to learn more about how you can customize each effect.Grow
Drag the slider to decide how much your element should grow on hover. Choose a starting point from which your element should start growing. By default, it starts growing from the center of the element but you can select a different point (e.g., top left).Shrink
Use the slider to adjust how much your element should shrink on hover. Choose a point towards which your element should shrink. By default, it shrinks towards the center of the element but you can select a different point (e.g., top right). Skew
Choose a skew direction: horizontal or vertical. Drag the slider to determine the angle in which your element should skew when visitors hover over it.  Choose a point from which your element should start skewing. By default, it starts skewing from the center of the element but you can select a different point.Rotate
Choose a direction for your element's rotation: clockwise or counterclockwise. Drag the slider to determine the angle by which your element should rotate when visitors hover over it. Choose a point around which your element should rotate. By default, the element rotates around the center but you can select a different point (e.g., top right).Combo
Grow / Shrink:

Use the toggle to enable or disable whether the box keeps its proportions. Use the sliders to adjust scale's width and height.   Rotate and Skew:

Use the slider to adjust the angle of the rotation. Use the sliders to adjust the degrees of the horizontal and vertical skew. Select a starting point for the skew effect.
