Wix Multilingual: Languages Available
Wix Multilingual supports over 180 languages per the ISO 639-1 standard. Supporting a language means you can Auto-Translate it with third-party services. However, not all languages are supported by these services. If your language isn't included, you can still translate your site manually.Languages available in Wix Multilingual:
