Associating Your YouTube Channel with Your Wix Site
Associating your YouTube channel with your Wix site lets you link to your site directly from the video using cards and annotations. To associate your YouTube channel with your site:Verify your site (domains) with Google Search Console.Add a card or annotation to your video which links to your site (step 2 in this article).
