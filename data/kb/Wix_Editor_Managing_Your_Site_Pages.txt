Wix Editor: Managing Your Site Pages
Manage your pages to keep your site sleek, fun and sophisticated. After adding a page, you can rename it, turn it into your homepage, hide it from your site menu and more. In this article, learn more about:
Managing your site pages Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Tip:Easily navigate to different pages by clicking the Switch Pages panel on the top bar of the editor.  Managing your site pagesManage your site pages at any point from the Pages & Menu panel in the editor. You can rename a page, duplicate a page to create a similar one, and delete those you don't need. This ensures your site remains user-friendly and up to date. To manage your site pages:
Go to your editor. Click Pages & Menu  on the left side of the editor. Hover over the relevant page and click the More Actions icon . Choose what you want to do: Rename the pageRename site pages to improve clarity and navigation for your visitors.
Click Rename. Type the new name of the page. Click Done. Duplicate the pageDuplicate site pages to save time and maintain consistency across your site.
Click Duplicate.  Choose from the following options: 
    
Duplicate Page: Create a duplicate of the page.  Make This Page Dynamic: Reuse this layout to create multiple site pages with content from a CMS (Content Management System) collection.   Copy and paste the page to another Wix Editor siteCopy and paste pages as needed between your sites, as long as both are in the Wix Editor.
Click Copy. Open the editor of the site you want to paste to. Paste the page by doing one of the following:
    
Right-click anywhere on the screen and select Paste. Right-click anywhere on the screen and select Paste & Apply Site Theme to apply the color and text themes of the target site.   Set the page as the site homepageClick Set as Homepage to make this your site's homepage. If you want to delete your current homepage, you must first set another page as the homepage. Then, you can delete the page that was used as your homepage. Hide the page from the menuClick Hide   to hide a page to keep it accessible for future use without displaying it in the site's navigation.Tip: To unhide, click the More Actions  icon next to the hidden page and select Show .Set the page as a subpageClick Subpage to nest this page under another page. This creates a subpage (drop-down) menu on your site. Visitors can see the subpages when they hover over the main page on the menu.
Tip:You can easily turn the subpage back into a main page at any time. Click the More Actions icon  next to the subpage and click Main page.Delete the pageDelete pages you no longer need to improve user experience.

Click Delete . Click Delete in the pop-up notification.
Note: If a page is linked to a Wix app (e.g., Blog, Stores, etc.), deleting the page also removes the app. Want to reorder your pages?Click the Reorder  icon next to the page name and drag the page to the desired location in the list.
 FAQsClick a question below to learn more. Can I change the settings of an individual page? Absolutely. You can change page settings, password protect individual pages and even create members only pages. Can I edit the width of my page?The width of your site is fixed at 980 pixels. You can design your site outside of the 980 pixel borders, however we recommend that anything that you add outside of these borders be mainly decorative and not vital content to your visitors' browsing experience.How can I delete an app page from my site?To delete pages associated with Wix apps (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Events, Wix Blog), you must delete the apps themselves to remove their pages.Can I hide a page without deleting it?Yes, you can hide a page from your site menu without deleting it. This is useful if you want to keep the page content but don’t want it visible to visitors. Can I add more than 100 pages to my site?You can add up to 100 static pages to your Wix site. The 100 page limit does not apply to dynamic pages, such as product pages, blog posts, and pages connected to your CMS (Content Management System).Why does my hidden page still appear when I Google search my site? Hidden pages still appear in search engines. If you don't want your site to appear in search results, you can prevent search engines from indexing your site. Learn how to hide a page from search enginesCan I download a page from my site?It is not possible to download a Wix site or any of its pages as the editor needs to be in constant connection with our servers. As such, it is not possible to work offline or save site pages to your computer.

Press Ctrl+P on your keyboard to use the print screen, or you can take a screenshot of the page and print it.
