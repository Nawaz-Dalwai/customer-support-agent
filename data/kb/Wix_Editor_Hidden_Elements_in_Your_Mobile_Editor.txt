Wix Editor: Hidden Elements in Your Mobile Editor
Wix automatically creates a mobile-friendly version of your site using the content from your desktop site. Elements may be hidden from your mobile site if they are placed outside the gridlines, or if there isn't sufficient space.Additionally, you can hide any desktop elements that you don't want to display on mobile and unhide elements whenever needed. Important:
This article refers to desktop elements in the mobile editor, not mobile-only elements. Any changes made to your mobile site do not affect your desktop site.In this article, learn more about:
Viewing hidden elements Hiding an element Unhiding a hidden element Finding missing elementsViewing hidden elementsClick the Hidden on Mobile  on the left side of the mobile editor to view your hidden elements. In this panel you can see elements that were automatically hidden, as well as elements that you choose to hide from your mobile site. Hover over a hidden element to see how and where it would appear on the mobile site. This is useful when considering whether to show it or not on mobile. Tip:Pinned elements from desktop may be hidden on your mobile site. You can find and unhide them from the Hidden on Mobile panel, but they are not pinned on your mobile site. Hiding an elementYou can hide any element from your mobile site. After hiding an element, it's automatically added to the Hidden on Mobile panel where you can access it at any time.To hide an element:
Click the element in the mobile editor. Click the Hide Element icon .Tip:You can also hide an element by pressing Delete on your keyboard.Note:Hiding desktop elements on your mobile site does not delete them. If you want to delete an element, you must do it from the desktop editor. Unhiding an elementYou can always re-add hidden elements to your mobile site. To show a hidden element:
Click Hidden on Mobile  on the left side of the mobile editor. Click the Show Element icon  on the relevant element.Note:You can unhide elements with hover interactions, however they appear without the hover effect on mobile.Finding missing elementsIf you can't find an element in the Hidden on Mobile panel, there may be another reason it isn't displayed.Click the options below to go through the troubleshooting steps and locate the element.Step 1 | Look for hidden grouped elementsIf you don’t see the element you are looking for under Hidden on Mobile, look for strips, groups, or container boxes. These components can contain multiple elements grouped together.  Click the Show Element icon  to unhide it to see if the relevant element is attached to one of them.Step 2 | Look through the elements on your pageIf the element is not listed in the Hidden on Mobile panel, it means it is showing on your page and could be hidden behind another element.
1. Use the layers panel to view the elements on your page.
2. Locate the element in the list.
3. Click the element and move it forward using right-click, the arrow keys, or the keyboard shortcuts:
To move the element forward: Ctrl + ↑ (Ctrl + Shift + ↑)  To move the element backward: Ctrl + ↓ ( Ctrl + Shift + ↓)Note: If you have hidden your header and footer, your elements will still show in the layers panel, but not on the page.Step 3 | Make sure your menu is not hiddenYou may have decided to hide the header and footer on a specific page of your mobile editor. If your pages have the header and footer disabled, the mobile menu is not displayed.Step 4 | Make sure the element hasn't been added to the menu boxIf you have edited your mobile site recently, you may have moved the element to the menu box. To move the element out of the menu box:
Open the menu box in the mobile editor. Right-click the element. Click Move to. Click where to move your element.
