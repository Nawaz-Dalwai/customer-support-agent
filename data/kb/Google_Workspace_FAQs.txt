FAQs: Google Workspace and your Wix accountClick on the arrows next to each FAQ to see the answers to these common questions.Can I transfer a Google Workspace business email from my Wix account to Google?Yes, you can transfer a Google Workspace business email from Wix to Google. This action disconnects the Workspace business email from your Wix account. To do so, contact Wix Customer Care for assistance. This is an irreversible action. Once transferred to Google, you cannot repurchase the business email through Wix.FAQs: Google WorkspaceClick on the questions below to be taken to Google Workspace help center to view answers.
