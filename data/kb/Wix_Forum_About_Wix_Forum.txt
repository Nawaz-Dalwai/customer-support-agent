Wix Forum: About Wix Forum
Wix Forum allows you to create an online community right on your website, where guests and members can start discussions, share posts, and interact with each other. By adding a forum, you enhance visitor engagement and keep them coming back for more dynamic interactions. For example, a cooking school could use Wix Forum to create a space for students to share recipes, discuss cooking techniques, and post photos of their culinary creations, creating a vibrant community of food enthusiasts and enhancing the educational experience.Create your own communityWix Forum allows you to create an online community right on your website, making it simple for visitors to start discussions, post questions, and share their thoughts. Set up is straightforward: just add Wix Forum to your site and customize it to match your style. Your visitors can become members, engage with each other, and become a part of your website’s community, all without needing any external platform.Ready to get started?Learn how to add Wix Forum to your site.Customize the design to fit your brandTailor the look and feel of your forum to match your website’s design. Choose from various layouts and design options to enhance your guests' experience and maintain brand consistency. You have control over colors, fonts, and how posts are displayed, ensuring the forum feels like an integral part of your site.Learn more about designing your forumModerate with easeMaintain the quality of your forum with moderation tools that allow you to manage posts and member interactions. You can approve or delete posts, pin top discussions, and set up guidelines that help keep the forum constructive and positive. Additionally, you can assign moderators from your community to help manage the activity.Learn more about moderating your forum.Boost engagement with member profilesMembers can create their own profiles, follow each other, and be followed. This feature encourages interaction and return visits, as members can receive notifications about replies and new posts in threads they are interested in. Profiles also make the community more engaging and personal.Learn more about member profiles.Optimized for mobileYour forum is automatically mobile-friendly, so users can participate in discussions, post questions, and interact with other members seamlessly from any device. They can use your site's member app to interact on the go which ensures that your community remains active and inclusive, regardless of where members are accessing your site from.Improves your site's SEOWix Forum is designed with SEO in mind, helping your site gain more visibility and traffic. Forums encourage the creation of fresh content, which can improve your site’s SEO ranking. Additionally, visitor-generated content can target keywords that might be missed in your main site content.FAQ'sClick a question below to learn more about Wix Forum.What pages are added to my site when I add Wix Forum?Two main pages are added to your site.
Forum Page: Your main forum page, where forum posts appear. Members Page: On this page, forum members can view other members of the community, follow each other, see what other members have written - even chat with one another.How do I access the forum to manage it?To manage the forum as an owner, log in to your live site using the email associated with your Wix account.What can guests, members and owners do on the forum?
Guest: As a guest you can read, like, and share posts from a forum. Member: As a member you can comment, post, and report inappropriate content. Owner: As an owner you can manage categories, move and delete posts, disable comments and pin a post to the top of a category. What languages is Wix Forum available in?Wix Forum is available in multiple languages.
