Wix Media: Free Wix Media Terms of Use
The free Wix media inside your Media Manager and on your template are royalty free images that we have provided for your convenience. You can use any of these images on your Wix site without breaking any international copyright laws. Note:Free Wix images are available for digital use within Wix ONLY. For more information, please see the Wix Terms of Use. Be careful with images found in external URLs. These images are not owned by Wix so you need to contact the author for permission to use them on your site. Learn how to add free Wix Media.
