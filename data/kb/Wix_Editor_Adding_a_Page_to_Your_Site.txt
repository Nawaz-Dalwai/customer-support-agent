Wix Editor: Adding a Page to Your Site
Add pages to create an informative and interesting site for your visitors. There are a variety of page types available, so you can find whatever you need to build your online presence. Each page type also has a range of preset design options with useful elements to help you expand your site quickly and easily. In this article, learn more about:
Adding a new page Available page templatesAdding a new pageChoose from a variety of professional pre-designed pages offered by Wix, or start from scratch with a blank page. Any new page you add is automatically added to your site menu.To add a new page:
Click Pages & Menu ﻿ on the left side of the Editor. Click Site Menu. Click Add Menu Item at the bottom of the panel. Select New page. Select either a blank page or a template:
    
Add a blank page: Click + Blank Page at the top left. Choose a template: Hover over the design you want to use and click Add Page.   Enter the new page name in the Pages panel. Click Done. Design the new page to your liking.Available page templatesPre-designed pages include all the elements you need, organized in a sleek layout that is easy to manage. Our templates are organized by category so you can easily find the right page for your site.Select an option below to learn more about the available template types.AboutUse these pages to introduce yourself and your business, and make a personal connection with your visitors. Give them a good perspective on who you are by sharing your resume, story, or team. ServicesUse these pages to give an overview of the services that you provide. Services pages are equipped with advanced layout tools, such as strips, repeaters and forms. You can add and remove these items as needed, and customize them to fit your site's look and feel. ProjectsUse these pages to showcase completed projects and those you're currently working on. You can choose from a number of layouts, depending on whether you want to include image descriptions and text.ContactUse these pages to inform your visitors of all the possible ways to contact you. Contact pages include elements like a contact form, social links and a Google Map in case you want to pin your physical location. 
GeneralUse these pages for any kind of purpose. General pages feature many different layouts and elements that you can tailor to your site's needs. Some pre-built elements include galleries, buttons, and text boxes.404 error pageAdd and adjust a custom 404 error page. These are the pages your visitors see if they click a link to your site that doesn't work. You can even customize it to suit your site's theme and feel.Important: You need a Premium Plan in order to create a custom 404 error page.Landing pageAdd a landing page to your site to keep your visitors updated and informed on your brand. You can choose from a range of options such as "Coming Soon" and "Down for Maintenance" pages. Some built-in elements include forms and app store buttons.StoreAdd a store to your site to display and sell products. Store and member pages are automatically added to your site, so visitors can view your products and keep track of their orders. Some of the pages added include "My Orders", "Shop", and "Cart".RestaurantsAdd Wix Restaurants to showcase and build your restaurant site. The app includes multiple restaurant pages to allow visitors to view your menu and order their favorite dishes online.BookingsAdd Wix Bookings to your site so visitors can view, book, and pay for your services in one place. The app includes booking pages and members pages, so you and your visitors have everything you need.
BlogAdd a blog to your site to share news, thoughts, and opinions with your visitors. Wix Blog comes with a blog page that showcases all of your categories and posts, and a page that displays singular posts.Pricing PlansUse Pricing Plans to showcase your subscriptions, packages, and memberships. It includes a page to display your services, as well as members pages for your visitors to manage their account and subscriptions.Dynamic pagesDynamic pages are an easy way to display data from your CMS (Content Management System) collections. Use them to showcase any repeating content, such as property listings and portfolios. The presets include a collection, as well as a dynamic list page, and a dynamic item page.Did you know?You can easily navigate to different pages by selecting the Switch page panel on the top bar of the Editor.
