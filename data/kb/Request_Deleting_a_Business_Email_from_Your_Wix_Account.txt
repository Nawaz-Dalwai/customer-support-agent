Request: Deleting a Business Email from Your Wix Account
Currently, there is no way to completely delete a business email, which has already expired, from your Wix account. The subscription will still be visible in case you want to repurchase that business email in the future.We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
