Wix Editor: Facebook Like Button vs. Facebook Page Like Box
Learn the difference between the Facebook Like button and the Facebook Page Like box:​Facebook Like Button:The Facebook Like button lets your visitors share your site with their Facebook friends. Their Facebook friends can see that they have liked or recommended your site. The button counts the site visitors that click it on your site.
