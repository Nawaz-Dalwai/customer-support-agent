Wix Gift Cards Request: Purchasing Gift Cards with Other Products
Currently, when customers purchase Wix Gift Cards, they need to pay for the gift card purchase separately. It's not possible to add gift cards and other products to the cart and pay for them together. We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
