Online Programs: Approving Requests to Join Your Program
When you create a private program, members of your site must submit a request to join and access the content. Important:
You must upgrade your site so people can start joining your program.  If you've set a price for your program, new participants are automatically approved once they make the payment. To approve requests to join your program:
Go to Online Programs in your site's dashboard. Click the relevant program. Click Manage next to Participants. Click the Pending tab. Click Approve next to the member(s) you want to approve.
