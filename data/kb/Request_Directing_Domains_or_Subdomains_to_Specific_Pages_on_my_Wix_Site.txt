Request: Directing Domains or Subdomains to Specific Pages on my Wix Site
Currently, it is not possible to direct a domain or subdomain to a page on your Wix site. It is only possible to connect a domain or subdomain to your site's home page. We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
