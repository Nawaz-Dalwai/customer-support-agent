Connecting PayItMonthly as a Payment Provider
Accept payments in the United Kingdom using PayItMonthly as your payment provider. Using PayItMonthly, your customers can pay in installments.General Info Supported countriesUnited KingdomSupported currenciesGBPSupported payment methodsCredit cards, debit cards and bank transferTransaction feesFrom 3% per transactionNotes
The currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your PayItMonthly account. If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined. Connecting PayItMonthly Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Connecting PayItMonthly
Go to Accept Payments in your site's dashboard.
Tip: Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it. Click See More Payment Options. Click Connect next to PayItMonthly.
Depending on whether you are creating a new PayItMonthly account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:Have an account
Enter your access_key and secret.
Tip: To find your credentials in PayItMonthly, Go to Admin>Developers>Create API User>Show secret access key. Click Connect.Don't have an account
Click Create PayItMonthly account. Sign up to PayItMonthly.
