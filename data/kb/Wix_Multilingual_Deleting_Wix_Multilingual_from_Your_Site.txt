Wix Multilingual: Deleting Wix Multilingual from Your Site
If you no longer need the Wix Multilingual app on your site or wish to change your site's main language, you can completely remove it. Deleting the app will permanently erase all editor translations, and translations from any Wix apps you have installed. Before removing Wix Multilingual, consider these precautionary measures:
Duplicating your site so you'll have access to your previous translations, site design and layout. Exporting your translations so that you can reference or import translations back to your site after re-adding Wix Multilingual.In this article, learn more about:
Deleting Wix Multilingual FAQsDeleting Wix Multilingual from your siteYou can delete Wix Multilingual if you're sure you want to permanently remove all the translations from your site. You'll be taken to your editor to finish deleting the Wix Multilingual app if your site has visual elements that were translated.DashboardWix EditorStudio Editor
Go to Manage Apps in your site's dashboard. Click the More Actions icon  next to Wix Multilingual. Select Delete.
(Optional) If the app has visual elements on your site, click Go to Editor.  Enter a reason for deleting and click Delete App. Publish your site so changes go live.
Go to your editor. Click Add Apps   on the left side of the editor. Click Manage Apps at the bottom. Click the More Actions icon  next to Wix Multilingual. Click Delete. Click the Why are you deleting this app? drop-down and select a reason. (Optional) Type any additional feedback in the Details field. Click Delete App. Publish your site so changes go live.
Go to your editor. Click App Market  on the left.  Click Manage Apps at the bottom of the panel. Click the More Actions icon  next to Wix Multilingual. Click Delete. Click the Why are you deleting this app? drop-down and select a reason. (Optional) Type any additional feedback in the Details field.
