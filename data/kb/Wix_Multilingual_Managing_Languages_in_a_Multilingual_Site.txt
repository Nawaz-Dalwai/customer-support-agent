Wix Multilingual: Managing Languages in a Multilingual Site
Use the Multilingual dashboard to manage all your languages in one place. As your business grows, add and translate more languages. You can also choose which languages to show on your site and remove the ones you don't need.In this article, learn more about:
Adding languages to your site Showing or hiding languages on your site Deleting languages from your site Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Adding a language to your siteWith Wix Multilingual, you can add multiple languages to your site, and manage them from your dashboard. To add a language to your site:
Go to Multilingual in your site's dashboard. Click + Add Language.
Select the language you want to add from the Language drop-down. Select a flag to represent the language from the Flag drop-down. Choose how you want to start translating: Manual translation or Machine translation.  (If available) Click the Make site in {language} visible right away drop-down and choose an option: 
    
No, keep it hidden for now: Keep the new language version hidden from your live site for now. You can unhide it later.  Yes, make it visible right away: Make the new language version of your site available so visitors and search engines can view it. You can do this later if needed.    Click Add Language.Showing or hiding a language on your siteAny time you add a secondary language, it is hidden by default. When you finish translating that language and are happy with how it looks, unhide it to show it to the world.To show or hide a language:
Go to Multilingual in your site's dashboard. Click the Status drop-down next to the relevant language and select an option:
    
Visible: The language is accessible to site visitors and appears in the language menu. Hidden: The language is not accessible to site visitors (even with a direct link) and does not appear in the language menu.  Deleting a language from your siteWhen you delete a language, its translations and any changes you made to that language are permanently deleted.To delete a language:
Go to Multilingual in your site's dashboard. Click the More Actions icon  next to the language you want to delete. Select Delete language. Click Delete to confirm.FAQsClick a question below to learn more about managing your site languages:Is my language menu still visible if I hide all my secondary languages?If you set all secondary languages to "Hidden", the language menu does not appear on your live site.My languages are set to visible, but the language menu is missing from my site. What happened?There are a few reasons why your language menu may be missing from your live site or editor. Read our Troubleshooting Guide to learn some common reasons why a language menu may be missing, and the way to solve each possible issue.Can I change my site's main language? Currently, it is not possible to change your site's main language to a different language. If this is a feature you'd like to use, vote for it here.Try these workarounds:
Delete Wix Multilingual and re-add it. This allows you to start from the beginning and choose a new main language. We recommend duplicating your site before deleting Wix Multilingual. This will help you later on when you re-add Wix Multilingual. You can refer to the duplicate for your previous translations and site design/layout.
Note: When you delete Wix Multilingual and re-add it, your purchased word credits will not be restored, and you may need to purchase more word credits to automatically translate your site. You can enable the Auto-switch language toggle, and automatically redirect visitors to the language version of your site that matches their browser's language.
