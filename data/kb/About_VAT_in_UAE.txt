About VAT in UAE
From October 18th, 2021, Wix.com started charging Value Added Tax (VAT) on our services to customers in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), in accordance with the Common VAT Agreement of the States of the Gulf Cooperation Council. You’ll see this tax added to your next renewal payment.If you’re a VAT-registered business in UAE, add your VAT number to your Wix account to get a tax exemption on your next payment. You can add your VAT number to your Wix account by updating your payment details. The exemption will apply from the next payment after that date.Updating your payment detailsUpdate your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ tax ID to get your tax exemption.To update your payment details:
Go to Premium Subscriptions in your Wix account. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant subscription. Click Update payment method. Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click Next.  Click Edit Details. Re-enter your card details and add your VAT Number to the VAT ID field.  Click Update. FAQsClick a question below to learn more about tax in the UAE.What is a VAT ID?Value Added Tax ( VAT) is a tax on the consumption or use of goods and services. A VAT ID is a 15-digit tax ID issued to businesses and organizations resident in UAE. What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect VAT on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
