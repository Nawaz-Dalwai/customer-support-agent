Wix Groups: Adding and Managing Your Group Members
Foster an online community around your business. Add people to your group to create a shared space for networking, collaboration, and discussions. Assign group admins to help monitor activity and grant members permission to create their own groups.In this article, learn more about: 
Managing members  Customizing members settingsManaging membersIn the members tab you can add members to your group, view a member's profile, review answers to membership questions, set group members as admins, and remove members. DashboardWix appLive site

Click + Add Members on the right.  Choose how you want to add members to your group: Send email invites, add existing site members, or copy a sharable link to send. Tip: Add your group's invite link to an email marketing campaign and send it to people you'd like to join your group.Review answers to membership questionsIf your group is set to private, when potential members want to access it, they need to request to join. You can set up membership questions to examine if you wish to approve the pending requests or not. You can also review the answers even after you approved the request.To review membership questions for pending members: 
Click View All next to Member Requests.  Click Review Answers next to the relevant pending member.  Review the answers then choose an option: Approve or Decline.To review answers by existing members:
