Wix Analytics: About Your Accounting Reports
View all the financial figures of your business and keep up to date with bookkeeping. The Wix Analytics accounting reports summarize your transactions and sales. You can also view a report for clients who have overdue payments.Note:Depending on your business you may see different reports. For example, you will only see the Stores Sales Summary report if you have Wix Stores on your site.How do I access my accounting reports?
Go to Reports in your site's dashboard. Scroll down to the Accounting Reports section. Select the relevant report.The types of accounting reportsClick a report to learn more about it.Stores sales summaryBreak down your Wix Stores sales by order. View order specific data such as gross sales, shipping, total sales and refunds. You can also export your sales data for specific time periods for accounting purposes.Note: This report is only available with Wix Stores.Transactions summaryGet a breakdown of the payment status of all sales as well as other payment figures for bookkeeping including:
Gross sales: The item price multiplied by the number of items sold. Tax Discount Total sales: The total amount of money expected from sales before deducting refunds, shipping and fees. Refunds Total revenue: Total sales minus refunds and chargebacks.Clients with overdue paymentsKeep track of clients and accounts with outstanding payments as well as the amount that is overdue.
