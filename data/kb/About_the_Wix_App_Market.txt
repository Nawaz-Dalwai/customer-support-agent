About the Wix App Market
The Wix App Market is filled with over 800 popular web apps, powerful services, and great features to enhance your website and grow your business. You can choose from apps made by Wix such as Wix Stores and Wix Blog, or from one of the many third-party apps. Most of the apps are free to use, mobile optimized, and can be added to your site in just a few clicks. Some apps have both a free and premium version while others require a premium upgrade.
