Hopp by Wix: Managing Your Pro Plan
Once you've upgraded your Hopp plan to Pro, you can manage your billing details and view your invoices at any time. In this article, learn more about: 
Managing your billing details Viewing your invoices Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Ready to upgrade? If you have not yet upgraded, learn more about upgrading your Hopp plan to Pro. Managing your billing detailsManage all payment and billing details for your Pro plan, including updating your payment method and how often you get billed. To manage your billing details:
Go to hopp.co and log in.  Click Settings on the left.  Click Upgrade under Manage Hopp Plan. 
Click Update payment method. Edit your payment details in the Update Payment Method section. Click Update on the bottom right.Viewing your invoicesOnce you have upgraded your Hopp account to a Pro plan and have started to get charged for it, you can view your invoices at any time. To view your Hopp invoices:
Go to hopp.co and log in.  Click Settings on the left. Click Manage Plan. Click View invoices. FAQsClick a question below to learn more about Hopp by Wix Pro plans. How can I upgrade my Hopp plan to the Pro plan? To upgrade to a Pro plan:
Go to hopp.co and log in.  Click Upgrade on the top right.  Select the type of plan you want to purchase:
    
Monthly: You will be billed once monthly. Yearly: You will be billed once annually. Purchasing a yearly plan will mean you get a discounted price.   Click Upgrade on the bottom right. Enter your card details in the Checkout section. Click Submit Payment at the bottom. Click Get Started to being using your Pro features.How can I cancel my Hopp Pro plan auto-renewal? If you wish to cancel your Hopp Pro plan, you can do so by following the steps below: 
Go to hopp.co and log in.  Click Settings on the left. Click Manage on Wix under Manage Hopp Plan.  Click the More Actions icon  next to your Hopp plan.  Click Cancel Hopp.
