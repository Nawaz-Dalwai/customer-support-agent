Wix Domains: Canceling Your Domain
You can cancel your domain at any time from your Wix account if you no longer need it. This turns off the auto renew feature, so you'll still own your domain until the end of the subscription period after you cancel.

Canceling your domain Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Canceling your domainYou can cancel your domain at any time from your Wix account.
Go to Premium Subscriptions in your Wix account. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant domain. Click Cancel domain. If you want to cancel your Premium or Studio plan, you'll need to do that separately. What happens when my domain renews at the end of the subscription period?To ensure your site remains live at all times, domains purchased from Wix are set to automatically renew 30 days before the end of their subscription period.
