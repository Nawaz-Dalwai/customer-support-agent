Connecting Pay-Easy Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Connecting Pay-Easy (through KOMOJU)
Go to Accept Payments on your site's dashboard.
Tip: Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it. Click See More Payment Options. Click See Options next to Pay-Easy.
Click Connect next to KOMOJU. Select an option:I have a KOMOJU account
Enter the Secret Key you received from KOMOJU.  Click Connect.I don't have a KOMOJU account
Click Create new account. This will redirect you to KOMOJU where you can create your new account.  Follow the instructions to sign up.  (When your account is approved) Repeat steps 1-4 and follow the steps under 'I have a KOMOJU account'. I have already connected KOMOJU and I want to add another payment method
Sign in to your KOMOJU account. Add the payment method you wish to connect and confirm that the payment method is active.  Go to Accept Payments in your site's dashboard.  Click Manage next to KOMOJU Enable the toggle next to your desired payment method.
