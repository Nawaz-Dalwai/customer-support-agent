ADI: Customizing Your Menu For Your Mobile View
You can customize the way your menu looks in the mobile view. Visitors viewing your site from a mobile device have different screen sizes and devices. You can customize your site’s menu so that it looks great on mobile devices, as well as on desktop.Important:Any changes made in the mobile view do not affect the desktop view of your site.To customize your menu:Step 1 | Add the elements to your menu on desktopYour desktop site is automatically converted to a mobile-friendly site. In order to customize your mobile view, you must first edit your menu on desktop. Click the header on your desktop site. Click the Show/Hide iconnext to an element to show it or hide it.Selected: The element or text field is shown. Unselected: The element or text field is hidden.Step 2 | Go to your mobile view
Hover over the desktop icon on the top bar of ADI. Click Mobile.
Step 3 | Customize your menu on mobileClick the header.Click the Show/Hide iconnext to an element to show it or hide it in the mobile view.              Step 4 | Change your section designClick the header.Click Section Design.Select a new mobile section layout.
