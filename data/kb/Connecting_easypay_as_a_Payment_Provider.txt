Connecting easypay as a Payment Provider
Accept payments from your customers using easypay as your payment provider. easypay allows you to accept payments through Referência Multibanco, MBWay, Visa & Mastercard cards, Bank Transfers, Universo Flex (Buy Now Pay Later) and Santander Consumer Finance. General Info Supported countriesPortugal and SpainSupported payment methodsReferência Multibanco, MBWay, Visa & Mastercard cards, Bank Transfers (Virtual IBAN), Universo Flex (BNPL) and Santander Consumer FinanceTransaction feesFees upon requestNotesThe currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your easypay account. If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined. Connecting easypay Frequently asked questions (FAQs)Connecting easypay
Go to Accept Payments in your site's dashboard.
Tip: Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it. Click See More Payment Options. Click Connect next to easypay.
Depending on whether you are creating a new easypay account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:Have an account
Enter your App ID and App KEY. Click Connect.Tip
To retrieve your credentials from easypay:
Login to easypay Backoffice. Generate and copy your App ID and App KEY.Don't have an account
Click Create easypay account. Fill in the registration form to create your account. Login to easypay Backoffice. Generate and cop your App ID and App KEY.
