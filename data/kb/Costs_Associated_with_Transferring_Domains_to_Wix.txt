Costs Associated with Transferring Domains to Wix
When you transfer a domain to Wix, you are required to purchase a minimum of one additional year of domain registration. Wix does not charge any fees for transferring your domain to Wix. You can view the total price of transferring your domain before completing the purchase. Notes:Costs for registering domains vary depending on many factors. View the price of registering your domain before transferring in the Extend step of the transfer process. View TutorialYou can use your free domain voucher when transferring your domain to Wix. Learn MoreIn the Privacy step of the transfer process, you can select Private Registration for an additional fee. Learn more about domain privacy.When you transfer your domain to Wix, your domain retains any time remaining on its current registration.
