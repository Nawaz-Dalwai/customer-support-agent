Translating Your Cookie Consent Banner Using Wix Multilingual
A cookie consent banner is a notification that informs your visitors on how their cookies are being used on your site. Make sure everyone understands your cookie banner by translating it into different languages. This helps your international visitors know about cookies on your site, and it's also a good way to follow privacy rules. With Wix Multilingual, you can translate your banner into the languages you've set up, ensuring that visitors from different regions are well-informed about your site's use of cookies.Step 1 | Add Wix MultilingualFirst, add and set up Wix Multilingual. You can add Wix Multilingual from your editor, your site's dashboard, or from the Wix App Market. Step 2 | Display a cookie consent bannerOnce you add the banner and publish, a banner instantly appears on your site which you can customize at any time.To display a cookie consent banner:
Go to Privacy & Cookies in your site's settings. Click Get Started. Enable the Display a cookie consent banner on site toggle. (Optional) Click Save & Publish to display the default banner on your site.Step 3 | Translate your cookie bannerAfter you have enabled your cookie consent banner and added your languages, you can begin translating your cookie banner. To translate your cookie consent banner:
Go to the Translation Manager in your site's dashboard. Click Cookie Consent Banner.
Choose how you want to translate your cookie consent banner:Auto-translationAutomatically translate your text using third-party machine translation services. Learn more about Auto-translation.
Review the translations.  (Optional) Replace the text in the text box to match the correct translation and click the Checkmark icon  to confirm the change.  Repeat these steps for each text you want to translate automatically. Manual translationTranslate your cookie consent banner manually, row by row.
Click the field of the text you want to translate and type your translation, or click the Translate icon  to automatically translate. Once you have reviewed the translation, click the checkmark to save your changes. Repeat these steps for each text you want to translate manually. 5.  Click Save & Publish.Step 4 | Check your translationView your site as it appears in other languages and check that all your languages are available to international visitors by selecting the secondary language from the language dropdown. FAQsClick a question below to learn more about the cookie banner.Where can I see my cookie consent banner? The cookie consent banner is visible on your published site. It cannot be seen in preview, or within the Wix Editor. You can also see a small preview window when setting it up. Can I rely on auto translate? Auto Translate is suggested as a tool for your convenience only. The translations should not be considered exact. Be sure to check if the translations meet your compliance requirements. Learn more.
